Independent RPGs live or die on first impressions, and few studios have planned theirs as carefully as 81monkeys. The World of Anterra Kickstarter is the team's bid to fund a nonlinear, open-world RPG built by a small crew with big-studio credits — and it doubles as the clearest look yet at how the game actually plays. If you are weighing whether the World of Anterra Kickstarter deserves your money, the good news is that the campaign is unusually detailed about combat, dialogue, lore, and exactly what each reward tier contains.

## What the World of Anterra Kickstarter Is Funding

According to [the official World of Anterra Kickstarter campaign page](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/81monkeys/world-of-anterra), 81monkeys spent more than three years shaping the world, narrative, and mechanics before moving into full production, and has now been in full development of the game for over a year. During that stretch the studio also shipped three other projects, including work-for-hire. That background matters: it explains why the campaign reads more like a design document than a wish list.

The pitch is a highly interactive, nonlinear open-world RPG with deep progression, fast party-based combat, and dense lore, wrapped in an interface designed to stay out of the way. The team describes itself as industry veterans with more than 25 years of combined experience and over 30 launched titles across casual, console, and social games.

## How Anterra Plays: Combat, Dialogue, and Exploration

### Streamlined grid combat

Combat looks like a traditional party-based tactics game, but 81monkeys insists it is the most streamlined grid system the team has built. Low-risk fights can be blasted through with a single button like an action game, while bosses and difficult opponents demand real positioning. If you dislike where a character plans to move, you simply walk them to a better tile before attacking.

### Inquiry-based dialogue

Instead of long linear conversations, Anterra uses a retro-inspired system modeled on classics like Ultima and modernized for current expectations. You ask questions in any order, and you can type your own words. That opens up riddles, mysteries, secrets, and easter eggs, and it keeps text short without sacrificing the narrative. Community reports from developer firesides suggest this is the system the team is most eager to show off.

### A real-time overworld

The overworld map does not pause. Enemies can hunt you, so running and hiding becomes a genuine survival tactic. Every step is a distinct location you can zoom into for gathering, hunting, fighting, or pursuing objectives — and you can walk between locations instead of using the map at all. Handcrafted sites like a hermit's cave or bandit camp are blended into procedurally generated terrain, and each save is seeded so your journey is unique.

## Lore, Regions, and the Four Magics

Each region of Anterra is guided by a core tenet — a principle that shapes local culture, history, and professions. Tenets are framed less as virtues and more as ways of overcoming the inherent challenges of being human. The land's older faith, Antheism, treats spirituality as grounded and nature-focused, with deities living in the natural world and followers informally called Ants.

Magic is divided into four disciplines, each tied to a natural vibration of the cosmos. Powerful mages once blended these vibrations using reagents from different regions, but all were banished for upsetting the world's balance, and mixing disciplines is now strictly forbidden. Ancient races — the fearsome Gorians, the secretive Faeries, and dragons known mainly by their giant bones — round out the history.

| Discipline | Cosmic vibration | Regional flavor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Power | Force and might | Centered in its own region, granting locals unique abilities |
| Growth | Expansion and life | Tied to the land's natural cycles |
| Affinity | Connection and bond | Shapes local culture and professions |
| Flow | Movement and change | Linked to water, weather, and motion |

## World of Anterra Kickstarter Reward Tiers and Add-Ons

Rewards are stacked: each tier includes everything below it, with one exception noted on the page. 81monkeys has also said it prefers stretch goals that do not gate in-game content, so the game ships with its planned feature set regardless of how far funding goes.

| Reward level | Highlights |
| --- | --- |
| Digital copy | The game on PC, Mac, or console of your choice, all stretch goal rewards, and a Discord backer title |
| Alpha access | Adds PC alpha demo access, a digital backer plaque, and backer wallpapers |
| Digital collection | Adds a digital map of Anterra, an art book, a lore compendium, and a DRM-free backer soundtrack |
| Plus-one | Adds a second game copy on any platform and a second alpha demo key for a friend |
| Playtest | Adds PC playtest access to in-development builds and private Discord feedback channels |
| Founder | Your name as a town founder, a statue in your game, a mini-quest, and new town dialogue |
| Hero | A region-level version with a statue hidden somewhere in the region and a hand-drawn map to find it |

The physical add-on, Merlin's Loot Sack, bundles a cloth map, a wax-sealed story letter, a Church of the Eye medallion, a letter from the game director, and a collector's box. Note that the Hero tier excludes the Founder's Pack, so backers who want both statues should read the fine print carefully.

## Platforms, Languages, and Multiplayer Co-Op

Anterra is being built from the ground up for both console and desktop, and the interface philosophy carries into item management: you can move, equip, trade, or drop items without opening a single menu. Character customization includes layered outfits with no gender restrictions, visually distinct weapon sub-types, and sheathed versus wielded states.

| Feature | What the campaign states |
| --- | --- |
| Launch platforms | PC, Mac, and consoles, with backers choosing their preferred platform |
| Alpha demo | PC only, for select backers |
| Playtests | PC in-development builds, expected to be rough |
| Localization | Professional translation at launch, plus community translation tools |
| Co-op | Drop-in and drop-out world hopping, shared adventures, and trading |
| World events | Rift-triggered events with their own progression and unlockable rewards |
| Design priority | Single-player must shine before multiplayer is layered on top |

## Practical Tips Before You Back

- **Check tier stacking first.** Almost every level includes previous rewards, but the Hero tier deliberately drops the Founder's Pack.
- **Plan for PC if you want early builds.** The alpha demo and playtest access are PC only, even if you intend to play at launch on a console.
- **Expect rough playtests.** The studio is explicit that in-development builds will not be feature-complete; you are there to give feedback.
- **Know how statues work.** Founder and Hero statues appear only in your own game, but you can share them so friends can add them to their worlds.
- **Follow the Developer Firesides.** Community reports point to regular Discord sessions, with recordings archived in the server's fireside-recordings channel.
- **Treat stretch goals as extras.** They add backer value rather than changing the shipped game, so do not back expecting feature unlocks.

For player experience with the campaign itself, the Discord community is the fastest source of updates, since the team posts design discussions and answers questions there between announcements.

## FAQ

**What exactly is the World of Anterra Kickstarter funding?**
It funds full production of 81monkeys' nonlinear open-world RPG, including its combat, dialogue, lore, localization, and console and desktop versions.

**Which platforms can backers choose?**
The campaign commits to PC, Mac, and consoles, with each backer selecting a preferred platform at launch. Early alpha and playtest builds are PC only.

**Do stretch goals add new in-game content?**
No. The studio prefers stretch goals that add backer value without gating gameplay, so the game launches with its planned features either way.

**Is World of Anterra multiplayer?**
Yes, but co-op is designed to sit on top of a strong single-player experience, letting players hop in and out of each other's worlds, trade goods, and trigger rift-based world events.
