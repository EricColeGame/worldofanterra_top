### Exploiting Weaknesses

Weakness systems reward preparation over raw stats. Before a big fight, ask what the enemy is vulnerable to and whether your party can actually hit that vulnerability. If only one character carries the right answer, your whole strategy is one bad turn away from collapsing. Spread coverage across the party.

The official description calls out environmental mechanics as a differentiator, so treat the battlefield as a tool rather than a backdrop. Player experience with tactical RPGs suggests the same habit everywhere: scan the arena before you commit your first action, then ask which hazard, elevation, or obstacle can be turned against the enemy.
