## Steam Support and Wider Platform Plans

The [World of Anterra Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2402470/) currently lists **Windows** support; its macOS and Linux flags are false. These fields describe the Steam version. They do not establish the platform plans for other stores or consoles.

The [developer's official website](https://www.anterrarpg.com/) explicitly describes the project as **Desktop & Console**. The readable homepage checked on October 29, 2026 does not name individual console models. Confirm the platform named in a current developer announcement or pre-order option before buying a particular edition; do not infer PlayStation, Xbox, or Nintendo release dates from the PC listing.

## Published PC Requirements

Steam's checked minimum requirements list Windows 10, 4 GB RAM, DirectX 11, and 6 GB storage. Its recommended requirements list Windows 10 or 11 and 8 GB RAM. These are published specifications for an upcoming title, not performance measurements from our testing. Consult the store page for the full CPU and graphics requirements before comparing a PC.

## Controllers and Steam Deck

Steam lists full controller support and Steam Input support. That does not by itself establish Steam Deck verification or guarantee performance on a handheld. The macOS and Linux flags likewise do not prove whether a compatibility layer will work. Use a current compatibility assessment when one becomes available.

## Release Status

As of the check above, Steam marks the game coming soon with a 2026 window. No specific day is given in that date field. These platform listings should not be read as confirmation that a released download is available now.
