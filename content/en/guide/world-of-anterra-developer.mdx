Every open-world RPG is shaped long before launch by the people making it — and the World of Anterra developer has been shaping this one for years. 81monkeys, a small independent studio staffed by industry veterans, is the World of Anterra developer behind a nonlinear RPG that pairs retro-inspired dialogue with a real-time overworld. ## Who Is the World of Anterra Developer?

The World of Anterra developer is 81monkeys, an indie team that has treated this RPG as its flagship project since the studio was founded. Rather than juggling a dozen prototypes, the team spent more than three years preparing Anterra's world, narrative, and mechanics before moving into full production. By the time the crowdfunding campaign went live, that full-production phase had already passed the twelve-month mark.

What makes that timeline unusual is what happened alongside it. The studio also completed three other projects during the same period, including work-for-hire contracts. That is a familiar survival pattern for independent teams: paid contract work funds the passion project without compromising it.

## The Credentials Behind the World of Anterra Developer

When the World of Anterra developer describes itself as a "small team with big dreams," the second half of that phrase is doing real work. Team members collectively bring more than 25 years of experience and have contributed to over 30 launched titles, with credits spanning *Hearthstone*, *Outer Wilds*, *Bomberman Live*, and the *Spyro the Dragon* GBA trilogy.

Those credits are not just resume filler — they map directly onto what Anterra is trying to do.

That combination explains a lot about the pitch. Anterra is aiming for the exploration freedom of a modern open-world game, the tactile feedback of a well-tuned card game interface, and the pick-up-and-play friendliness of a classic action title — all at once.

## How the World of Anterra Developer Approaches RPG Design

Most of the World of Anterra developer's public design commentary centers on removing friction without removing depth. The studio has repeatedly framed its choices as reactions against modern RPG conventions.

| Convention | The Anterra Approach |
|---|---|
| Dialogue buried in walls of text | Inquiry-based conversations where you ask the questions |
| Menu-heavy tactical combat | Streamlined grid combat with a single-button option for low-risk fights |
| Endless menus and lists | In-world items and intuitive icons instead of UI panels |
| Purely handcrafted or purely generated worlds | Handcrafted locations blended into procedurally generated terrain |
| Static overworld map | A real-time overworld where enemies actively hunt you |

**Combat** looks like a party-based tactics game, but the team has stressed that it is deliberately simple to operate. You can blast through routine encounters with minimal input, then slow down and use positioning, party composition, and enemy knowledge when a boss demands it. Disliking your planned move is not a problem — you can simply walk your character to a better spot before attacking.

**Dialogue** is the studio's signature experiment. Instead of picking from four canned responses, you ask questions — in any order — and can even type your own. The team cites retro RPGs like *Ultima* as inspiration, modernized for contemporary expectations. Typing words also unlocks riddles, mysteries, and hidden secrets.

## What the World of Anterra Developer Has Revealed About the Game

Across developer updates, the World of Anterra developer has outlined a world with genuine internal logic. Each region is guided by a core tenet — a belief system that shapes local professions and culture rather than serving as flavor text.

| System | What Has Been Shared |
|---|---|
| Exploration | Oceans, continents, castles, towns, caves, and dungeons; sailing is confirmed |
| Magic | Four disciplines tied to cosmic vibrations: Power, Growth, Affinity, and Flow |
| Religion | Antheism, a nature-focused faith whose followers are called Ants |
| Populations | Six distinct human cultures plus ancient races: Gorians, Faeries, and Dragons |
| Side content | Fishing, hunting, cooking, crafting, building, treasure maps, faery hunts, detective work |
| Multiplayer | Co-op "hopping" between worlds, trading, and rift-triggered world events |
| Presentation | Dynamic lighting with multiple simultaneous shadow-casting light sources |

Community reports from the studio's Discord firesides suggest the team treats feedback as the main variable in the project. Notably, the studio has said it prefers stretch goals that do **not** gate in-game content — the game is meant to ship with its planned features intact, and backer feedback is what refines it.

Character customization deserves its own mention. Items can be layered in nearly any combination, there is no gender segregation of gear, weapon sub-types look visually distinct, and equipped items animate differently depending on whether your character is walking or running.

## FAQ

**Who is the World of Anterra developer?**
81monkeys, an independent studio made up of industry veterans whose collective credits include *Hearthstone*, *Outer Wilds*, *Bomberman Live*, and the *Spyro the Dragon* GBA trilogy. The team has been building the world and narrative for more than three years.

**What platforms will World of Anterra release on?**
The studio has committed to PC, Mac, and consoles, with backers able to choose their preferred platform at launch. Early demo and playtest builds are PC-only.

**Does World of Anterra have multiplayer?**
Yes. Co-op is designed to layer on top of the single-player experience, letting players hop in and out of each other's worlds, adventure together or separately, and trade goods. Rift events tied to the narrative add their own progression and rewards.

**How can I keep up with the World of Anterra developer?**
Join the studio's Discord for Developer Firesides, check the archived recordings channel, and follow campaign updates. Backers at higher tiers also get access to alpha and in-development playtest builds.
