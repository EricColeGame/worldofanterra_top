## Current Release Window

As checked on October 29, 2026, the [World of Anterra Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2402470/) marks the game **coming soon** and gives **2026** as its release window. The checked Steam date field does not provide a month or day. It is a store listing, not a guarantee that the schedule will remain unchanged.

World of Anterra is an open-world RPG developed by **81monkeys**. Its announced features include non-linear quests, party customization, and grid-based combat with quick-time actions. Those are developer descriptions of an upcoming game; they are not findings from a finished-game review.

## Where to Check for Changes

Use the Steam page for its latest store status and wishlist option. The [developer's official website](https://www.anterrarpg.com/) also links to the project's community and pre-order page. Check the date and context of an announcement before treating it as the current schedule.

## Pre-order Does Not Mean a Released Download

The official website advertises pre-orders through BackerKit. A pre-order offer does not establish that a finished build is available to play. Check the offer's delivery terms and the developer's current announcements before purchasing. This page does not provide an installer or claim access to a playable build.
